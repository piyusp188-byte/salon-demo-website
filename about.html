About Choice Beauty Salon

Our Story & Philosophy

A welcoming sanctuary dedicated to helping every woman embrace her authentic radiance.

Home > About Us
Women-Owned Business

Created by Women, for Women

Located in the heart of Ghatlodiya at Marutinandan Complex, Choice Beauty Salon was founded with a clear purpose: to give local Ahmedabad women an upscale, warm, and impeccably clean space where beauty services are performed with genuine care and technical excellence.

Over the years, we have built a thriving community of regular clients through honest consultation, hospital-grade hygiene, and mastery of advanced techniques like Nanoplastia hair smoothening and skin-revitalizing therapies.

Our Core Pillars

The Principles Guiding Every Appointment

We measure our success not by sales, but by the genuine smiles of the women who walk out our doors.

Honest Consultation

We never recommend unnecessary services. We assess your hair texture, scalp condition, and skin type, offering honest recommendations tailored to your goals.

Uncompromising Hygiene

Our regular clients frequently highlight our cleanliness. We use single-use waxing strips, sanitised scissors, fresh capes, and clean towels for every client.

Continuous Craft Mastery

From latest European Nanoplastia innovations to classic Gujarati bridal draping, we stay trained in modern techniques that prioritize hair and skin health.

Our Space

Step Into Serenity

Designed with soothing warm terracotta and rose gold hues, relaxing seating, and dedicated private sections for facial therapy and waxing.

We want your visit to be the most relaxing hour of your week. Come in for a quick clean-up or spend an afternoon with our transformative hair spa rituals.
